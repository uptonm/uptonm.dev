I'm Mike Upton, a Boston-based software engineer currently contracting for Meta, where I build the OSS/BSS/NMS systems that map and route their extensive global telecom footprint. My interests live mostly in the web space — React, TypeScript, and increasingly Rust — and in interfaces that feel considered rather than clever.

Away from the keyboard, you'll find me snowboarding, traveling, at concerts and festivals, or making a bit of music of my own.

01 — Feature

On the work

Over the last several years I've helped build a platform that manages Meta's global telecom footprint — from advanced fiber-optic pathfinding with PostGIS and pgRouting to a map-based project-management system deployed across the United States and Europe.

Along the way I've helped lead standards and code reviews, and guided a team's migration from Go to Rust as our primary service language. I care about performance you can feel and interfaces that get out of the way.
